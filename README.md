# Manada 纠正 — Mandarin Correction Engine

An AI-powered Mandarin correction tool for HSK 1–4 learners. Speak or type in English or Mandarin and get instant feedback on tones, grammar, vocabulary, and sentence structure — with a score out of 10.

---

## What It Does

- **English → Mandarin**: Type what you want to say in English and get the correct Mandarin translation
- **Correct my Mandarin**: Type or speak a Mandarin sentence and get it corrected with a full breakdown
- **Scorecard**: Grammar, Tones, Vocabulary, and Structure each scored out of 10
- **Breakdown table**: Side-by-side comparison of what you said vs what is correct, and why
- **Play pronunciation**: Hear the corrected Mandarin spoken back to you
- **Voice input**: Speak directly into the app using your mic (Chrome and Edge)
- **History**: Last 5 corrections saved automatically in your browser

---

## How to Use

1. Open the app link in Chrome or Edge
2. Choose your mode — English to Mandarin or Correct my Mandarin
3. Type or speak your sentence
4. Click Correct it
5. Review your score and breakdown
6. Tap Play pronunciation to hear the correct Mandarin

## Tech Stack

- Pure HTML, CSS, and JavaScript — no framework, no build step
- Open-source AI models via OpenRouter (Google Gemma, Meta Llama)
- Web Speech API for voice input and text-to-speech output
- Google Fonts — Noto Serif SC for clean Hanzi display

---

## HSK Scope

This tool covers HSK 1 to 4 vocabulary and grammar only:

- HSK 1-2: Basic sentence structure, negation, simple measure words, question particles
- HSK 3: Ba sentences, comparison with bi, resultative complements, reduplication
- HSK 4: Complex ba and bei, conjunctions, potential complements, shi...de emphasis

---

## Privacy

- No backend server
- No data stored anywhere except your own browser
- Correction history saved in browser localStorage only
- AI calls go directly from your browser to OpenRouter
